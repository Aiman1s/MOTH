What's inside

Sixteen games and a vault of party tools.

TWENTY

Two players each build a five-item lineup on a $20 budget, taking or giving items turn by turn. Then the group decides who won.

The Imposter Pack

Word Imposter, Question Imposter, Music Imposter, Higher / Lower and Hot Take. Everyone is in on it except one person. Work out who.

Featured games

Wavelength, Charades, Hot Potato, Heads Will Roll and Blockbuster. Guess where a clue sits, act it out, or pass fast before the timer blows.

Party classics

Most Likely To, Truth or Dare, Two Truths & a Lie and Spin the Bottle, with the app keeping things moving.

Sell It

A Crowdy original. Two players pitch a ridiculous product and the room picks the better salesperson.

The Party Vault

Eight quick tools for any night: a decider wheel, coin flip, dice, bottle spinner, name picker, number picker, team splitter and timer.

How it works

One phone, three steps.

  1. Add your players

    Type everyone's name once. Every game draws from the same list.

  2. Pick a game

    Most games need three or more players, and six work with two. The app shows which ones fit your group.

  3. Pass the phone

    The app tells you who's up. Secret roles only appear after the right person taps to reveal them.

Privacy

No sign-up. Nothing to log in to.

Crowdy has no accounts. Here is what does and doesn't leave your phone.

  • What happens in a game stays on the phone. Player names and anything created during a game are kept on the device for that session and aren't sent anywhere.
  • The free version shows ads. Ads come from Google AdMob at natural breaks after a game. In the UK and EEA you're asked for consent before any personalised ads. A subscription removes them.
  • Purchases go through the store. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat managing access. We never see your card details.
  • Adult content is off by default. 18+ packs only appear if someone deliberately turns them on, and they are part of the paid tier.
  • Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
Questions

Before you ask.

Is Crowdy free?

Yes. Every game is free to play, with ads shown between games. A subscription, billed weekly or yearly, removes the ads and unlocks extra packs and all 18+ content.

How many people do I need?

Three or more for most games. TWENTY, Blockbuster, Charades, Most Likely To, Truth or Dare and Spin the Bottle work with two.

Do we each need a phone?

No. Crowdy is pass-and-play on a single phone. Nobody else needs to download anything.

Does it need the internet?

The games themselves play offline. Purchases, subscription checks and ads need a connection.

Is Crowdy the same app as Rabble?

Yes. Rabble became Crowdy in 2026. Same games, same app.

Is it on Android?

Yes. Crowdy is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.
