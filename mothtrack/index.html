Privacy

Your records stay on your iPhone.

MothTrack has no account to create and no ads. Here is exactly what does and doesn't leave your device.

  • Stored on your device. Your accounts, balances, notes and history are kept on your iPhone. We don't run a server that receives them, and we can't see them.
  • No bank connection. MothTrack doesn't link to your bank or sync transactions. You enter the figures.
  • Purchases go through Apple. Membership is handled by Apple and RevenueCat, which process purchase information to manage access. Your financial records aren't part of that.
  • Backups are yours to keep. Export a password-protected backup whenever you like and store it where you choose. We can't recover lost records for you, so it's worth doing.
  • Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
Questions

Before you ask.

Does MothTrack connect to my bank?

No. There's no bank connection, Open Banking link or automatic syncing. You record your balances yourself, or import them from a spreadsheet.

Where are my financial records stored?

On your iPhone. They aren't uploaded to us. They only leave your device if you choose to export a backup.

Can I import an existing spreadsheet?

Yes, MothTrack can import XLSX and CSV files. Spreadsheets vary a lot, so you map the columns and review the values before anything is saved.

Can I track assets and debts together?

Yes. Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe, so both sit side by side, including property, pensions, loans and overdrafts.

Are projections guaranteed?

No. Projections are illustrations based on assumptions you set. Real outcomes will differ. MothTrack is a tracking and planning tool and doesn't give financial advice.

How much will it cost?

MothTrack will be a paid app with monthly, annual and one-time lifetime options, and no ads. Eligible new subscribers will be able to start with a free trial. Prices will be shown on the App Store at launch.

How do backups work?

You export a password-protected backup file from the app and keep it wherever you like. There's no automatic cloud backup, and we can't restore your records or a forgotten backup password.

When will it launch?

Soon. It's being tested now and we haven't set a date.
