2. Crowdy

Crowdy's core gameplay runs on your device and does not require an account. We do not ask for or directly collect your name, email address, contacts, or precise location through Crowdy. Purchase and advertising services do transmit limited data from the app as described below.

  • There are no accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.
  • Anything you do in the app, such as the players you add or content created during a game, stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. This information is temporary and exists only for your play session.
  • Crowdy's games can be played without an internet connection, but purchases, subscription checks, consent messages, and advertising require communication with third-party services.

2.1 Purchases

Purchases. Crowdy offers optional paid content through auto-renewing subscriptions, billed weekly or yearly. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store on iOS, and by Google through Google Play on Android. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscription status. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (transaction receipts, product identifiers, subscription status) on our behalf. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other information that identifies you, and it is not used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat processes this data as our processor under its data processing terms; see RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

2.2 Advertising

Crowdy uses Google AdMob to show interstitial advertisements to users who do not have an active premium entitlement. Premium users are not intentionally served advertisements. Ads appear only at natural breaks after a completed game, subject to frequency limits.

Crowdy also shows a second, optional format: a rewarded advertisement. In TWENTY and Blockbuster, saving a recorded clip to your device plays a short video first. You only ever see this one by choosing to save a clip, and if the advertisement cannot be shown, the clip is saved anyway.

Where required by law, including in the UK, EEA, and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before requesting personalised ads, and we request non-personalised ads if you decline. Elsewhere, including the United States, we may request personalised ads. On iOS, personalised advertising also depends on your App Tracking Transparency choice: if you decline, your advertising identifier is not used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

Non-personalised ads are not selected using your past behaviour, but Google explains that they can still use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device or app identifiers, such as an advertising ID, app-set ID, or app/developer-scoped identifier;
  • advertising data, such as ads shown and interactions with those ads;
  • product interaction data, such as app launches, taps, and video views; and
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs, launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

Google uses this information to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services, including analytics and fraud prevention. Data sent to Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit. We receive aggregated advertising reports and do not use AdMob to obtain your name or email address. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

2.3 Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law, including in the UK, EEA, and Switzerland, Crowdy uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present a consent message before requesting ads. Depending on your region and choices, Google may serve non-personalised or limited ads, or ads may not be available. Where the control is available, you can review or change your choice from Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in Crowdy. You can also reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device's privacy settings.

2.4 Retention

We retain only aggregated advertising reports for as long as reasonably necessary for reporting, accounting, fraud prevention, and service improvement. Google, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play retain data they control in accordance with their own policies and legal obligations. Because Crowdy has no user accounts, we generally cannot connect those third-party records to your name or email address.

2.5 Mature content

Mature content. Crowdy contains some content intended for adult players, hidden behind an optional toggle that is off by default. Crowdy is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through Crowdy, contact us using the details below.

4. MothTrack

MothTrack is a personal net worth tracker for iPhone. It is available on the App Store. MothTrack has no user accounts: there is no sign-up, login, or MothTrack account held on our servers.

4.1 Your financial records are stored on your device

The information you enter into MothTrack is stored on your iPhone. We do not collect or receive it: we do not operate a server it is sent to, and we cannot see, access, or restore it. This includes:

  • account names and types, balances, assets and liabilities, balance history, and contribution entries;
  • monthly notes;
  • your date of birth, which the app asks for during setup and uses only to show progress by age;
  • household labels and ownership choices (for example You, Partner, or Joint);
  • your reporting currency, check-in schedule, and reminder settings; and
  • projection assumptions and goals.

If you import a spreadsheet (XLSX or CSV), the file is read and processed on your device. Importing a file does not connect MothTrack to your bank or any other institution, and MothTrack does not connect to bank accounts.

If MothTrack cannot read a spreadsheet, it can give you a formatting prompt to copy into an AI tool of your choice. Copying this prompt does not send your financial records to an AI provider, and the copied text does not contain them. If you then choose to upload your spreadsheet to that provider, you are sharing its contents with that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. This is optional: you can simplify the file yourself or add your accounts manually.

4.2 Purchases

MothTrack is free to download. Continued access after setup and the guided tour requires MothTrack Pro, offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to check purchases and manage membership status. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (transaction receipts, product identifiers, and subscription status) on our behalf, together with the technical information needed to do so. We do not connect this identifier to your name or email address, although it does distinguish one installation from another, and it is not used for advertising. We do not send RevenueCat your account names, balances, date of birth, notes, or any other financial record. RevenueCat also gives us aggregated subscription reporting. RevenueCat processes this data as our processor under its data processing terms; see RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

4.3 Device features

  • Face ID, Touch ID, and passcode. If you turn on the app lock, authentication is carried out by Apple's system on your device. We never receive your face or fingerprint data or your device passcode.
  • Reminders. Check-in reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, at a time you choose. They are not sent from a server operated by us.
  • Widgets. Home Screen widgets read a limited summary (totals and dates) that the app shares with its own widget on the same device. This is not a cloud upload.
  • Institution logos. Logos are bundled inside the app. MothTrack does not look them up online or send your account names to a logo service. A logo identifies an institution; it does not mean that institution is affiliated with or endorses MothTrack.

4.4 Backups and exports

MothTrack does not send your records to us, and we do not run a backup service. Your records are included in your iPhone's own backup. If iCloud Backup is switched on, Apple stores a copy in your iCloud account; if you back up to a computer, a copy is kept on that computer. Those backups are made by your device under your Apple account and are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. We never receive them. You can also export a password-protected, encrypted backup file whenever you choose, including after a membership has ended, and restore from it later.

  • You decide where an exported file is saved or shared. If you save it to iCloud Drive or another provider, that provider's own terms and privacy practices apply to it.
  • We cannot recover your records for you. If you lose, erase, or replace your device, they can be restored only from your iPhone's own backup or from a backup file you exported. We also cannot recover a backup if you forget its password.

4.5 Advertising and analytics

MothTrack has no ads or advertising tracking. It contains no advertising SDK, and it does not include a general-purpose behavioural analytics SDK. The only third-party service in the app is RevenueCat, used for purchases as described in Section 4.2.

4.6 Removing your data

Because we do not hold your records, you control them directly. You can erase everything from inside the app (Settings > Delete all data), and deleting the app also removes the records it stored on that device. Please note that this is separate from four other things:

  • deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings;

  • it does not erase purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat, which they retain in accordance with their own policies and legal obligations.

5. ilmi

ilmi is an iPhone app for learning Islam through short lessons, reading the Quran, and prayer times. It is available on the App Store.

ilmi has no accounts, no sign-in and no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It asks for a first name, which you can skip and which stays on your device, and it never asks for your email address. The only third-party software in the app is RevenueCat, which is used to manage purchases as described in Section 5.5, and TelemetryDeck, which sends usage analytics as described in Section 5.8. Usage analytics is on by default for 18 or over, off by default for 13 to 17 or if no age is known, and always off for Under 13.

5.1 What is stored on your device

The following is stored on your iPhone:

  • your first name, if you give one during setup, which the app uses to greet you, including in reminders;
  • your other answers when you first set up the app: an age range, why you are learning, your interests, how much you already know, and your daily time;
  • your lesson progress, points, streak and streak freezes;
  • your reading position in the Quran;
  • the prayers you have ticked off;
  • your Medina; and
  • your settings, including your language and Quran translation choices.

The app never sends your name, your answers, your reading position or the prayers you tick to us or to anyone else. Your progress and your Medina stay on your device too, and we do not receive a copy of them. When usage analytics is on, some counts about them are sent, such as which lessons you finish; Section 5.8 lists them.

Like other app data, this is included in your iPhone's own backup if you keep one (for example iCloud Backup, which Apple stores under your Apple account and its terms). We never receive it. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small summary that the app shares with its own widgets on the same device, including your language and Quran translation choices, so the widgets show the same.

Share cards. ilmi can make a picture of your streak, a finished lesson or series, or your Medina. The picture is made on your iPhone and your name is not on it. It goes nowhere unless you send it from the iPhone's share sheet, and then it goes where you choose.

Ratings. ilmi may ask for a rating using Apple's own rating card. Apple decides whether the card appears and handles anything you submit.

5.2 Location, for prayer times

ilmi asks for your location only if you turn on prayer times. Access is "while using the app". Before it is used, your location is rounded to two decimal places, about one kilometre. That rounded location, together with your chosen calculation settings and the dates needed, is sent to aladhan.com (api.aladhan.com) so that it can calculate prayer times for where you are. As with any website, aladhan.com sees the request, including your IP address. We send it nothing else about you. The app also gives the same rounded location to Apple's location service, to find the name of your town to show above the times. Only the rounded location and that town name are kept, and only on your device. The qibla direction is worked out on your device and is not sent anywhere.

You can turn this off at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Without it, the rest of the app works as normal.

5.3 Audio

When you listen to a recitation that is not already on your iPhone, the audio for each verse is downloaded from everyayah.com, or from cdn.islamic.network if that does not work, and kept on your device until about a week after you last open that surah. The recording of the call to prayer is downloaded from cdn.aladhan.com. As with any website, the servers that provide these files see the request, including your IP address and which file was asked for. We send them nothing else, and we do not receive anything from them about you.

5.4 Notifications

Reminders and prayer alerts are optional. They are scheduled on your device as local notifications. A reminder may use your first name, and it is written on your device. There is no push-notification server.

5.5 Purchases

ilmi Plus is an optional subscription bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive or store your payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to check purchases and manage whether ilmi Plus is active. The app starts RevenueCat when it opens, whether or not you buy anything, and this does not depend on the usage analytics switch in Section 5.8. On our behalf, RevenueCat processes:

  • a random app user ID, which is created on your iPhone when the app is first opened;
  • your purchase history (transaction receipts, product identifiers, and subscription status);
  • Apple's identifier for vendor, a code Apple gives the apps from one developer on one device; and
  • technical information, such as the country of your App Store account, your device model, iOS version, app version and preferred languages, and, as with any server, your IP address.

We do not connect these to your name or email address, although they do distinguish one installation from another, and they are not used for advertising. We do not send RevenueCat your name, age range, the location used for prayer times, lesson progress, or anything else you enter in the app. RevenueCat also gives us aggregated subscription reporting. RevenueCat processes this data as our processor under its data processing terms; see RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

We process this to provide what you have bought (performance of a contract) and to understand how the subscription is doing (our legitimate interests). Purchase records are kept for as long as they are needed to maintain and restore your access, to deal with refunds and disputes, and to meet our legal record-keeping obligations.

5.6 Younger users

ilmi can be used by people of any age, including those under 13. It offers everyone the choice of giving a first name, which stays on the device, and it does not ask anyone for an email address. The purchase service described in Section 5.5 works in the same way for every user, whatever their age. The age range chosen at setup stays on the device. It is used to choose how lessons are shown and which are open, and to set usage analytics: on by default for 18 or over, off by default for 13 to 17, and always off for Under 13; see Section 5.8. Location is off unless someone turns on prayer times. Purchases can only be made by, or with the approval of, the holder of the Apple account. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, contact us using the details below.

5.7 Removing your data

Deleting the app deletes all the data it stored on that device, including downloaded audio. It does not cancel a subscription, which is managed in your Apple account settings; it does not remove copies already contained in a backup of your device; it does not erase purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat, which they retain in accordance with their own policies and legal obligations; and it does not remove usage analytics already sent (see Section 5.8).

5.8 Usage analytics

ilmi can send usage counts to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service from TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany. We use them to see where people stop during setup, how well the ilmi Plus screens work, and whether people keep learning. Whether this is on depends on your age range:

  • 18 or over: on by default. You can turn it off with the "Share usage counts" switch at You > Settings > Privacy.
  • 13 to 17, or if no age is known: off by default. You can turn it on with the same switch.
  • Under 13: always off, with no switch.

Nothing is sent before you answer the age question during setup. If you choose 18 or over, the setup pages you have reached so far are then sent. If you choose anything else, they are dropped. If you were already using ilmi and your age range is 18 or over, usage analytics starts after you update and stays on unless you turn it off. While it is off, nothing is sent and TelemetryDeck is not started. No feature of the app depends on it.

What is sent, when it is on:

  • a code for your phone: a salted hash of Apple's identifier for vendor, made on your iPhone. TelemetryDeck hashes it again on its server. The code stays the same on that phone, so events from one phone can be seen together. The app sets no other user ID;
  • the time of each event in UTC, which TelemetryDeck says it rounds to the hour, and a random code for each session;
  • which setup pages you reach, and whether you started with your plan or a series you chose, but not which series;
  • which lessons you start and finish, whether you passed, and which series you finish;
  • reaching your daily goal, streak milestones, and each Medina level you reach;
  • that you opened the Quran tab or the Prayer tab;
  • how the ilmi Plus screens are used: when one is shown, at what moment and whether you asked for it, the plan you choose, when a purchase starts, how a purchase or restore ends, and when you choose not now;
  • when the app is opened, that it is a new install, the date of your first session, and running totals TelemetryDeck's software adds, such as number of sessions, session length and days used; and
  • device details TelemetryDeck's software adds to each event, including device model, iOS version, app version, screen size, language, light or dark mode, accessibility settings such as text size and reduced motion, whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight, and calendar details such as the day of the week.

What is never sent to TelemetryDeck:

  • your name, or anything you type;
  • your age range, or anything else you answer during setup;
  • your location, your prayer times, or the prayers you tick;
  • which verses you read or listen to; and
  • your region, your time zone or your local hour. TelemetryDeck's software would normally add these, and the app overwrites them before an event leaves your phone.

The app never asks for the advertising identifier. As with any server, TelemetryDeck's servers see your phone's IP address when an event arrives. TelemetryDeck says IP addresses are never stored on its server. It may work out a country from the connection.

How to turn it off or on. Go to You > Settings > Privacy and use the "Share usage counts" switch. When you turn it off, sending stops at once and events waiting on your phone are dropped, though a batch already on its way may still arrive. You can turn it on again at any time. Nothing else in the app changes.

Under 13. Usage analytics is always off for anyone who chooses Under 13. There is no switch, and nothing is sent. If someone changes their age range to Under 13, during setup or in Settings, sending stops at that tap, but what was sent before then cannot be taken back.

What happens to it. TelemetryDeck says it treats what it receives as anonymous and does not act as our processor. It also says it has no schedule for deleting old events, and expects to delete them after 7 to 10 years without guaranteeing that. We do not try to work out who a code belongs to, and we have no way to find or remove one person's past events. Our basis for this is legitimate interests (understanding how the app is used so we can improve it), with a switch to turn it off.
