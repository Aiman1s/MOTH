What it does

A straight answer on every job you open.

An honest fit score

A score from 0 to 100 for how well your CV matches the role, with a clear verdict. It will tell you when a job isn't worth applying for.

Why you're a fit

The parts of your experience that match what the listing is asking for, so you know what to lead with.

Add to your CV

Specific things the role wants that your CV doesn't show yet.

Cut or fix

What to trim or reword for this particular job.

A cover letter for the role

Works where you already look

LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Indeed and other job boards, right on the listing page.

How it works

Install it once, use it on every listing.

  1. Add Bluntly to Chrome

    Install from the Chrome Web Store, create an account and add your CV.

  2. Open a job

    Browse listings as usual. When one looks interesting, run Bluntly on the page.

  3. Read the verdict

    See your score, why you fit, and exactly what to change before you apply.

Privacy

It only reads a page when you ask it to.

Bluntly needs your CV and the job listing to do its work. Here is how that is handled.

  • An account is required. We store your email and the profile and CV details you choose to add.
  • No passive browsing data. Bluntly processes a page only when you run it there. It doesn't read or collect your browsing in the background.
  • AI providers process your request. The job description and the relevant parts of your profile are sent to third-party AI services to generate the result. We choose providers whose terms don't allow your content to be used to train their models.
  • No advertising. We don't sell your data or use it for ads.
  • You can delete your account. Do it from the extension or by email, and we delete your personal data within 30 days, apart from billing records we must keep by law.
  • Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
Questions

Before you ask.

What does Bluntly cost?

It's free to use, with a paid Pro plan for more. Current prices are shown when you upgrade.

Which job sites does it work on?

LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Indeed and other job boards.

Why would I want a low score?

Because it saves you time. A handful of well-matched applications tends to beat a hundred that were never going to land.

Can I rely on what the AI writes?

Treat it as a draft. AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete, so read and edit anything before you send it to an employer.

Does Bluntly track my browsing?

No. It only processes a page when you actively use it there.

How do I cancel Pro?

From your account settings. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
