Crowdy
Is Crowdy the same app as Rabble?
Yes. Rabble became Crowdy in 2026. Same games, same app, now available as Crowdy on the App Store and Google Play.
Is Crowdy free?
Yes, it's a free party game. A paid tier expands the game content and removes any ads.
How many people do I need to play?
Any group in one room. It's pass-and-play on a single phone, so no second device needed.
Is Crowdy on Android?
Yes — Crowdy is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).
Bluntly
What is Bluntly?
A Chrome extension that scores your fit on any job you open, drafts a tailored cover letter, and shows what to add or cut on your CV.
What does Bluntly cost?
It's free to use, with a Pro tier for more. You add it from the Chrome Web Store.
